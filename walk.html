Octee City · walking
The town (menu)Octee Airlines

WASD or the arrow keys to walk. On a phone, use the buttons below.

  • E go into a shop, come out again, use a counter or shelf, call the Joelmobile or get on it.
  • Inside a shop, walk to the counter and press E to clock in. Walk to a shelf and press E to buy things (grocery, bakery, food court, night market). Log in first.
  • F full screen, and F again to leave full screen.
  • N night mode, and N again for day.
  • M big map with names, and M again to shrink it. I your bag.
  • Space jump. Climb Octee Peak, the biggest mountain in the north: a lit trail with steps and logs to jump over.
  • Octee Peak is a Mario-style hike: grab coins (some float over the logs), jump on goombas to squash them, and do not touch them. You have three hearts. Reach the flag at the top to win CCT, once a day (log in first).
  • Hills and mountains are solid: you stop at their foot. The highway goes through them in lit tunnels.
  • At a bus stop: B bus arrival times, E get on a bus that is stopped (and off again at a stop). K bus routes, anywhere. Buses run in loops from the Octee Central Bus Terminal.
  • H hide the map, and H again to show it.
  • The highway: walk or drive up a ramp from the ring road. Express buses 506 and 518 run on it and stop only at the ramps.
  • G at the Car Park (or inside it, at the counter) to buy a car. V to get in or out of your car and drive it.
  • Esc closes any panel.

Nothing here is to scale. The bank is not a real bank, and the police are not real police. The Joelmobile is not a real vehicle, though it is very real in the sense that it is always late.
