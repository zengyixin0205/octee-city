Octee City

Walk around the city (WASD)

A small town next to FIA with 24 buildings, a bank that pays interest every day, jobs, a police station, a Town Square chat, a Gazette, a Game Tower and games to play. Walking around is free. Working, the bank, the chat, the games and owning buildings need an Octee Airlines login.

Click a building on the map to see its jobs, property, reviews and games.

The buildings

  • OCT Bank: Keep your CCT here and it grows every day. Do not ask how.
  • City Hall: Where the stamps are. There are more stamps than there are citizens.
  • Clinic: Open all hours, staffed by people who have read a leaflet.
  • Fire Station: The fire is always somewhere else. The hose is always here.
  • Post Office: Everything posted here arrives. Eventually. Some of it arrives in the wrong town.
  • Bakery: Bread, buns and a cake with no name. It smells like an airport gate.
  • Library: Quiet, except for the shelf that talks about its overdue books.
  • Cinema: Showing the same film since the town was founded. Nobody has reached the end.
  • Power Station: Keeps the lights on for FIA and, sometimes, for everyone else.
  • Radio Tower: Octee Radio's city branch. The playlist is one song, on loop, very politely.
  • Food Court: Every dish is peanuts in a different hat.
  • Harbour: A harbour with no sea. The cranes are very committed anyway.
  • Police Station: Keeps the town orderly. Breaking the rules gets you arrested. Ask anyone who has tried.
  • Tax Office: Every peanut is taxed. Some peanuts are taxed twice.
  • Car Park: Four floors, zero spaces, all of them full.
  • Gym: Lifts things, puts them down, and says they were heavy.
  • Peanut Museum: One exhibit, a peanut, under glass. It is very valuable.
  • Airport Bus Stop: The bus to FIA. It goes everywhere except FIA.
  • Town Square: The chat room. Everyone talks, nobody listens, and the bakery reads every word.
  • Game Tower: Stack the blocks as high as they go. The tower leans, but that is not your fault.
  • Lost Property Office: Lost your fine? Come and claim half of it back. The form is very long, and the pen is on a string.
  • Laundrette: Everything comes out cleaner, except the socks, which were never in.
  • Swimming Pool: Thirty metres long, one metre deep, and the lifeguard has a very serious whistle.
  • Night Market: Stalls selling things you did not know you needed, at prices you will not believe.
Octee City is a parody town next to FIA. Accounts and money are the Octee Airlines ones; the town itself lives at this address.
